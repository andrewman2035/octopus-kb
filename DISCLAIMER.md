# Disclaimer

Octopus is software for personal use, provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. You run it on your own
machine, with your own accounts and keys, and you are responsible for how you use it.

## Platforms and content

- Respect the terms of service of every platform you connect to Octopus or ask it to read, and the rights of the
  people whose content you save. Saved copies are for your own reference.
- Octopus does not bypass logins, paywalls, rate limits or anti-bot measures. When a page cannot be read without a
  login, it asks you for screenshots or pasted text instead; the Chrome extension saves only what your own browser
  already shows you.
- Reading browser cookies is **off by default**. `youtube_cookies_from_browser` makes yt-dlp use your own browser's
  YouTube login; set it only if you accept that, and only for your own account.
- Audio and video are downloaded (yt-dlp; X videos directly) only to transcribe them locally; the download is not kept
  as a copy of the media.

## Chat platforms

- **WeChat ClawBot** works through Tencent's iLink bot interface (the HTTP protocol Tencent publishes in its open-source openclaw-weixin client), logged
  in by scanning a QR code with your own WeChat account. Tencent may change or restrict it at any time.
- Telegram, Feishu and Discord are used through their official bot APIs with a bot you create. Messages pass through
  those platforms' servers.

## Models

- The `claude:` models run the Claude Code CLI on **your own** Claude subscription. That use is subject to
  Anthropic's terms for your plan. Do not run Octopus on your subscription for other people: give them their own
  setup, preferably with an Anthropic API key (`anthropic:` models) or another provider.
- With an API provider, the text being summarized and your questions (with the matching parts of your notes) are sent
  to that provider under its terms. With `local` models nothing is sent.
- Answers are generated by a model and can be wrong, even when they cite your notes. Check the sources.

## Email

The email entrance saves mail only from your own addresses and only when the receiving provider authenticated the
sender (DMARC pass, or aligned DKIM / SPF for a domain without DMARC). That protection is only as good as the
provider's authentication results: use a dedicated Gmail mailbox (supported out of the box), send from a domain that
publishes DMARC, and do not let that mailbox fetch mail from other accounts. Other providers are supported only when
they write a trustworthy `Authentication-Results` header; QQ Mail and Outlook / Hotmail are not.

## Your data

All notes, the index, backups and logs stay in your data folder. Keep your own backups. Deleting a note moves it to
the `trash/` folder; deleting the data folder deletes everything.
